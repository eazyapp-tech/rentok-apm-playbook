# Candidate assessment process

**For:** Hiring managers, recruiters, and interviewers

Use the same core process for every candidate. Change only the questions needed to test a claim, gap, or risk specific to that person.

The process must answer:

1. Can this person take responsibility for unclear work and bring it to completion?
2. Can this person understand a Product problem and make a sensible decision?
3. Can this person turn a decision into clear work for the people involved?
4. Can this person learn, check their work, and change after useful feedback?
5. What is the right entry route if we hire them?

Using AI in Product work, Uxcel results, and technical ability are recorded separately. None of them can rescue weak evidence on the main job.

## Stage 1: application review

Read the email, resume, portfolio, and any linked work that matters.

For each important statement, mark it as:

- **Verified:** the source directly shows the fact.
- **Candidate claim:** the candidate says it, but the result or personal contribution is not yet checked.
- **Inference:** a reasonable possibility, but not a fact.
- **Missing:** important evidence that the application does not contain.

Create a short candidate evidence file. Do not treat a polished portfolio as proof of ownership before asking what the person did, what failed, and what happened after the first version.

## Stage 2: Uxcel Product Management baseline

Ask the candidate to complete the free [Uxcel Pulse Product Management assessment](https://uxcel.com/assess-skills) before the recruiter screen.

Uxcel currently uses 25 adaptive questions and normally takes about 25 minutes. Ask the candidate to follow Uxcel's own assessment rules and share the result or Skill Graph, not their account credentials.

Record:

- overall score
- reliability score, when shown
- strongest areas
- areas worth asking about

Do not use an automatic cutoff. A high score does not prove that the person can do RentOk's work. A low score does not automatically reject someone with strong practical evidence. Use the result to choose a useful follow-up question.

If the candidate cannot access the assessment, record the reason and continue when the rest of the evidence is worth assessing.

## Stage 3: recruiter evidence screen

**Owner:** Srijan or the assigned recruiter

**Time:** 30 minutes

Tell the candidate:

> We need someone who can take important Product work, make it clearer, keep the right people involved, and follow it until the user problem is solved.

Ask for specific past examples. When the candidate says “we”, ask what they personally owned.

### The three core questions

#### 1. Ownership of unclear work

Tell me about a piece of work involving several people that was unclear when it reached you and that you personally brought under control.

Useful probes:

- What did you do yourself?
- How did you track decisions, owners, dependencies, and unfinished work?
- Where did it get stuck, and how did it end?

#### 2. Completion after the first version

Tell me about the last thing you followed through to a real result.

Useful probes:

- What happened after the first version was ready?
- What did you test or check yourself?
- What remained incomplete, and who experienced the result?

#### 3. Judgment and learning

Tell me about a decision or piece of feedback that changed what you did.

Useful probes:

- What did you believe at first?
- What evidence or feedback changed your mind?
- What did you do differently afterwards?

### Using AI in Product work

Ask one short question:

> Tell me about a real task where AI helped you think, write, check, organise, or follow work through. What did you accept, reject, and verify?

If the candidate has little experience, ask where they would try it in everyday Product work and what they would check before relying on the result. Do not turn this into a test of tool names or prompt vocabulary.

### Candidate-specific question

Use no more than one extra question to test the largest claim or risk in the candidate's evidence file.

### When to stop

Do not send the work sample merely to complete the process. Stop when the application and screen give clear evidence that the candidate cannot meet the required ownership, communication, judgment, or learning level.

## Stage 4: RentOk Product work sample

**Independent work:** About three hours, with a hard limit of four hours

**Live discussion:** 60 minutes with Sanchay or the hiring manager

Give every continuing candidate the same [Booking to Tenant work sample](product-work-sample.md), product links, fictional evidence, time limit, and submission rules.

Use the [candidate work sample checklist](candidate-work-sample-checklist.md) to prepare access, run the time box, handle blockers, and record the cross-check after the exercise.

The candidate explores the real RentOk product for up to one hour within the four-hour limit. They may use notes, the internet, AI, and other tools. AI is optional and does not receive extra credit.

Before sending the exercise:

1. Check that the web, app store, and tutorial links open.
2. Confirm whether self-access works and that the candidate warning about automatic messages is still accurate. If self-access does not work or the candidate does not want to use personal contact details, prepare a clean individual test account.
3. Make sure any recruiter-provided account contains no real customer or candidate information. Disable outbound messages and payments before sharing it.
4. Make the recruiter responsible for access questions and reply within one business day.
5. Prepare the standard live-discussion fact in the private Notion hiring record. Do not publish it in this repository.

The candidate may ask up to three clarification questions. Use these standard replies:

- **Access is blocked:** Tell the candidate to pause the timer. Within one business day, provide a clean individual account or tell them to continue with the exercise evidence and the alternative observation instructions.
- **The answer is not in the exercise:** Say that the information is unavailable, ask the candidate to state an assumption, and continue.
- **The candidate asks for the preferred solution:** Say that there is no predetermined solution and that the reasoning matters.
- **The candidate asks about tools or AI:** Repeat that any tools are allowed, AI is optional, and the candidate remains responsible for the result.
- **The candidate reaches four hours:** Ask them to stop and submit what they have.

Record any additional answer that materially changed the information available to the candidate. Reuse it consistently for later candidates. Do not give one candidate a hidden product answer that others could not receive.

An access problem is not a candidate failure. Judge what the candidate tried, what they noticed, how they handled the blocker, and whether they separated observation from assumption. Do not reward finding the largest number of screens or settings.

Every candidate also submits the short working-method note in the exercise. Do not score it separately. Use it to understand the candidate's judgment, learning, honesty, checking, and use of tools.

Judge the work against the five required areas in the scorecard. Look for grounded exploration, a clear decision, sensible limits, a workable release path, and honest separation of observation from assumption. Do not use a hidden preferred solution.

### Before the live discussion

Read and score the written work before speaking with the candidate. Record direct evidence, open questions, and the largest risk. Do not let presentation quality substitute for Product evidence.

### Live discussion

Use the 60 minutes consistently.

#### 1. Opening and summary: 10 minutes

Briefly explain the call. Then ask the candidate to explain the problem, recommendation, and most important reason in five minutes.

#### 2. Reasoning and judgment: 15 minutes

Ask:

1. Which part of your recommendation are you least certain about?
2. Which assumption could most easily make it wrong?
3. What did you deliberately leave out of the first release?
4. What would you check first with access to the team and product data?
5. Where could this change cause harm or extra work?

#### 3. New information: 10 minutes

Give the candidate the standard new fact stored in the private hiring record. Use the same fact for every candidate assessed during the same hiring round. The fact should challenge an important assumption without teaching one preferred answer.

Ask:

> What, if anything, would you change in your recommendation?

Look for whether the candidate understands the new risk, changes the appropriate part of the plan, preserves sound parts of the original decision, and avoids expanding the release without reason.

#### 4. Execution: 10 minutes

Ask:

1. What is the first action you would take after this meeting?
2. What decision does Engineering need before starting?
3. What should Quality Assurance test that may be easy to miss?
4. What should Customer Success know before release?
5. How would you know within two weeks that the change is causing trouble?

#### 5. Working method and AI accountability: 5 minutes

Ask:

1. What did you verify independently?
2. What did you change or choose not to use while preparing the answer?
3. Which part of the submitted work would you not trust without more evidence?

If the candidate used AI, ask how they checked its work. Do not ask for private chat history or reward prompt performance.

If they did not use AI, record that honestly. Assess their checking, curiosity, and willingness to learn. Do not create a second AI exercise.

#### 6. Candidate questions: 10 minutes

Give the candidate a real opportunity to understand RentOk, the role, the team, and the possible entry routes.

### After the live discussion

Add the live evidence to your scorecard and record any score change with its reason. Do not erase the score recorded before the discussion. Do not calculate an average or change a weak required score merely because the candidate presented confidently.

## Stage 5: optional technical exercise

Use this only after the candidate meets the main job level and when a technical claim or possible strength is relevant to the decision.

Give the fictional booking data exercise. Allow any suitable tool. Ask for a usable action list, the method, the uncertain records, and instructions another person can follow tomorrow.

A strong result:

- identifies records that need action
- separates urgent work from later follow-up
- catches missing, invalid, duplicate, or conflicting data
- keeps uncertain records out of unsafe actions
- produces something another person can reuse
- explains assumptions and limits plainly

Do not reward complexity. A correct spreadsheet can be better than an unfinished application. Score only the technical method and reliability. AI ability is assessed through ordinary Product work, not through this exercise.

### Assessor check for the data exercise

Keep the row-by-row answer check in the private Notion hiring record. Do not publish it in this repository. Verify it against the current exercise file before use and score the candidate's method, reliability, assumptions, and handling of uncertain records.

## Stage 6: references and decision

Use the [candidate scorecard](candidate-scorecard.md). Each assessor records evidence and scores before discussing the candidate.

References should test the main risk found during the assessment. Ask questions such as:

1. What work did this person own without repeated reminders?
2. Tell me about a time they failed to finish or missed an important detail. What happened next?
3. How did they work when another team was slow or disagreed?
4. What kind of work would you not yet trust them to own alone?
5. What should their next manager do in the first month to get the best work from them?

Ask for examples. A general positive reference should not erase a specific concern.

## Decide fit before entry route

First decide whether the candidate fits the APM job.

Then decide the route:

- Recommend the internship intended to convert when the candidate meets the job bar but has limited evidence of consistent work in a real team and Product setting.
- Recommend direct full-time when the candidate meets the job bar and has repeated professional evidence of doing comparable work. This normally includes RentOk's probation terms.

Probation does not replace assessment. Internship should not be used to underlevel an experienced candidate.

AI experience and Uxcel results do not decide the entry route. Any exception to normal probation should follow company policy and have a clear reason.

### Decision aid

Use this to prevent AI evidence from becoming either an unfair gate or an unfair shortcut. It guides judgment but does not replace it.

| Overall evidence | AI evidence | Likely decision |
|---|---|---|
| Strong repeated Product experience | Limited experience, but curious and learns | Direct full-time, normally with probation |
| Strong repeated Product experience | Thoughtful and established use | Direct full-time, normally with probation |
| Strong early-career potential | Limited experience, but curious and learns | Internship intended to convert |
| Strong early-career potential | Thoughtful use already demonstrated | Usually internship, unless the overall professional evidence supports direct full-time |
| Weak ownership or Product judgment | Strong AI use | Do not hire merely because of AI ability |
| Strong Product evidence | AI ability not observed | Gather limited evidence if useful, but do not reject automatically |
| Any experience level | Repeated dishonesty, unsafe behaviour, or refusal to learn | Treat as a serious judgment or integrity concern |

If the final decision differs from this guidance, record the evidence and reason.

## Finish every assessment

Use the final decision note in the candidate scorecard. Record the evidence, main risk, missing fact, Uxcel result, AI capability, technical signal, entry route, and one next action.

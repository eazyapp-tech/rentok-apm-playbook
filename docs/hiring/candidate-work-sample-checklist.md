# Candidate work sample checklist

**For:** Recruiters, hiring managers, and interviewers

Use this checklist when preparing, running, and reviewing the [Booking to Tenant work sample](product-work-sample.md). It keeps the candidate experience safe and consistent without publishing private answer guidance.

## Before sending the exercise

- [ ] Open the current [work sample](product-work-sample.md) and confirm that it contains no private answer, scorecard, or assessor link.
- [ ] Open the web app, mobile-app link, [RentOk University](https://docs.rentok.com/), and the booking guide. Record any broken link before sending the exercise.
- [ ] Confirm the Stage 2 Uxcel result or recorded access failure is in the candidate's private record.
- [ ] Confirm the submission method, deadline, and person who will answer access questions.
- [ ] Prepare the standard new fact for the live discussion in the private Notion hiring record. Do not add it to this repository.
- [ ] Prepare the technical exercise answer check in the private Notion hiring record if that exercise may be used.

## Check product access

- [ ] Prefer a clean individual test account. Never give candidates a shared account.
- [ ] If a recruiter-provided account is used, confirm that it contains no real customer or candidate information.
- [ ] Confirm that recruiter-provided accounts have outbound messages and payments disabled.
- [ ] If a candidate uses self-access, tell them that RentOk may send automatic messages to contact details they control.
- [ ] Tell candidates never to enter another person's real contact details, make a payment, send a payment request, or manually send a message.
- [ ] Do not ask candidates to use a live operational account with existing customer data for hands-on creation.

### Prepare a recruiter-provided account

- [ ] The recruiter or hiring manager creates the account and records its owner in the private hiring record.
- [ ] Use only RentOk-controlled owner and contact details. Select a clean test property, or create one with fictional details. Check that the property, rooms, tenants, and payments contain no real data.
- [ ] Create the first fictional room before handoff. Some room-creation flows can send an automatic owner message, so verify that any message goes only to a RentOk-controlled contact.
- [ ] In the account and property settings, turn off the specific tenant and payment reminders, payment collection, and payment-request actions that the candidate could use. Check the saved state again before sharing access.
- [ ] Share access only through the team's approved private credential-sharing tool. If you are unsure which tool to use, confirm it with the hiring manager first. Never put credentials in this repository, an email thread, or the candidate-facing exercise.
- [ ] If any automatic message or payment safety check cannot be verified, do not ask the candidate to create data in that account. Let them use their own account with the stated safety rules, tell them to continue using only the written exercise information, or pause the exercise.

## At the start

- [ ] Repeat the time box: plan for about three hours, stop at four hours, and spend no more than one hour exploring RentOk.
- [ ] Repeat the four-page limit and the five required parts.
- [ ] Tell the candidate that AI and other tools are allowed, AI is optional, and the candidate remains responsible for the result.
- [ ] Tell the candidate that they may ask up to three clarification questions.
- [ ] Explain that waiting for an answer pauses the timer.
- [ ] Give no preferred solution or hidden product answer.

## If access is blocked

- [ ] Ask the candidate to stop the timer after 15 minutes of failed access.
- [ ] Within one business day, provide a clean individual account or confirm that they should continue without product access.
- [ ] If they continue without access, ask them to record what they tried, where they were blocked, and two things they would inspect with access.
- [ ] Do not penalise a documented access problem.
- [ ] Record the blocker and the reply so later candidates receive the same help.

## Review the written work

- [ ] Read and score the document before the live discussion using the [candidate scorecard](candidate-scorecard.md).
- [ ] Separate what the candidate observed from what they assumed.
- [ ] Look for a clear decision, sensible limits, a workable release path, and honest treatment of uncertainty.
- [ ] Record direct evidence and open questions. Do not use a hidden preferred answer.
- [ ] Preserve the written scores before the live discussion.

## Run the live discussion

- [ ] Use the 60-minute agenda in the [assessment process](assessment-process.md).
- [ ] Give the same standard new fact to every candidate in the same hiring round.
- [ ] Keep the new fact and any technical answer check in private Notion.
- [ ] Record what changed after the discussion and why.
- [ ] Do not let presentation confidence, Uxcel results, AI use, or technical ability erase weak evidence on a required score area.

## Close the assessment

- [ ] Complete the [candidate evidence file](../../templates/candidate-evidence-file.md) and [candidate decision note](../../templates/candidate-decision-note.md) in the candidate's private Notion page.
- [ ] Record the entry route and the reason for it only after the main job bar is judged.
- [ ] Record any access problem, timing issue, or candidate question that affected the experience.
- [ ] After three to five candidates, review actual time spent, access blockers, candidate feedback, and whether the exercise separated stronger and weaker judgment.
- [ ] If the reusable process changes, update the decision log and changelog before using the new version.

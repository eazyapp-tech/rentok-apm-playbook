## Decision rule

Do not use an average score to hide a weak required area.

For `Yes` or `Strong yes`, each required area must score at least 3. There must be no serious concern about honesty, reliability, or willingness to do necessary routine work. The interview and work sample should tell a consistent story.

Use `Hold` only when one specific missing fact could change the decision. Write the fact and the person who will check it.

## Entry route

Choose the entry route after deciding that the candidate fits the job.

- **Internship intended to convert:** use when the candidate shows the required potential but still needs to prove consistent performance in a real team and product setting.
- **Direct full-time with normal probation:** use when the candidate shows repeated professional evidence of doing the same kind of work at the required level.

Do not use probation as the reason to skip evidence. Do not use an internship to underlevel an experienced candidate.

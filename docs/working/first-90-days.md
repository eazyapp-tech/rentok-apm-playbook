# The first 90 days

**For:** New APMs and the Product Lead

This is the normal starting plan for an early-career joiner.

An experienced direct hire may move faster. They should not skip learning RentOk's users, current product, and important business rules.

## First 30 days: learn and become dependable

The main goal is to understand how RentOk works and become reliable on bounded work.

- Learn the main users, product areas, and customer journeys.
- Learn how Product, Design, Engineering, QA, Customer Success, and Sales work together.
- Review current product work and the sources used to track it.
- Spend time with Customer Success and real customer problems.
- Triage issues with guidance.
- Keep selected work current and handle straightforward product questions.
- Take part in UAT and learn how releases are checked.
- Own one small piece of work from problem to follow-up.

**Evidence at 30 days:** bounded work can be trusted without repeated reminders.

## Days 31 to 60: own a workstream

The main goal is to manage most of the product work around one problem or feature.

- Structure the problem and gather the needed evidence.
- Prepare the product material that Design and Engineering need.
- Handle normal questions and keep decisions current.
- Notice blockers, dependencies, and scope changes early.
- Run product testing for the work.
- Prepare the release and follow early user feedback.
- Bring recommendations, not only questions.

**Evidence at 60 days:** the Product Lead does not need to join every conversation about the work.

## Days 61 to 90: begin owning an area

The main goal is to take responsibility for a small product area or connected group of problems.

- Find and describe problems without waiting for a task.
- Recommend what should happen first and what should wait.
- Make safe and reversible product decisions.
- Handle most normal product work independently.
- Understand the customer and business effect of the decision.
- Improve an existing workflow, not only add a feature.
- Review what happened after release and recommend the next step.

**Evidence at 90 days:** the person can own a small area while keeping the Product Lead informed at the right points.

## Internship conversion

The internship is intended to lead to full-time work. The conversion decision should be based on observed evidence, not only potential or time served.

Before recommending conversion, ask:

- Did the person own bounded work through testing and follow-up?
- Did they keep commitments without repeated chasing?
- Did they make work clearer for other people?
- Did they show sound judgment when information was incomplete?
- Did they learn from feedback and need less help over time?
- Can Design, Engineering, QA, and customer-facing teams work with them well?
- Do both RentOk and the person want to continue?

If one important area is still unproven, name the missing evidence and set a clear check. Do not hide it inside a vague extension.

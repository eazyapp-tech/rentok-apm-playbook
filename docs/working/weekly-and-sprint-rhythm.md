# Weekly and sprint rhythm

**For:** APMs, the Product Lead, and teams working with them

The exact routine will change with the work. The basic habits should remain.

## Each day

Start by checking:

- What is moving?
- What changed?
- What is blocked or at risk?
- What needs a decision?
- What will be missed if nobody acts today?

During the day:

- answer product questions
- follow important dependencies
- triage new issues
- work on assigned product problems
- review Design or development when needed
- keep the current decision and status easy to find

Before finishing:

- write down important changes and decisions
- give each meaningful blocker an owner and next step

The tools should describe the work. Updating the tools is not the result by itself.

## Before a sprint

For work that may enter the sprint, check:

- the problem and user
- the current decision and scope
- the latest design
- important states, rules, and acceptance checks
- dependencies and risks
- questions that still need a decision
- owner, priority, and expected release

Do not send unclear work into development merely to fill the sprint.

## During a sprint

- Stay close enough to answer small questions before they become delays.
- Keep changes to scope or behaviour visible.
- Raise meaningful risk early.
- Follow the parts Product owns.
- Check the developing product against the decision, not only the ticket.

## Before release

Check:

- QA and Product testing
- critical defects
- product acceptance
- known limits
- internal preparation and user communication
- rollout and rollback needs

## After release

Check:

- whether people can use and understand the change
- support questions and customer feedback
- new defects
- usage or outcome measures that matter
- whether the original problem improved
- what should happen next

## Weekly review with the Product Lead

Use five short sections:

1. **Completed:** what reached a meaningful result?
2. **Moving:** what is progressing normally?
3. **At risk:** what may fail or be delayed?
4. **Decision needed:** where is the Product Lead's judgment needed?
5. **Learned:** what changed our understanding?

The review should help decisions and action. It should not be a reading of Jira.

Use the [weekly review template](../../templates/weekly-review.md) to prepare the discussion.

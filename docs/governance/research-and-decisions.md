# APM hiring research and decision log

**For:** Playbook maintainers and hiring decision makers

This page records why material hiring rules exist. It is not a list of companies to copy and it is not a candidate assessment page.

## How to use this log

Add or change an entry only when the role, assessment, entry route, or expected way of working changes.

For a foundational decision:

1. Define the question.
2. Prefer current, direct sources.
3. Use at least two relevant sources when practical.
4. Separate what the sources show from what RentOk infers.
5. Record the decision, guardrail, and reason to revisit it.

Do not research ordinary wording choices. Stop when the evidence is sufficient to make a reasoned RentOk decision.

## Current decisions

### The APM owns execution and grows into wider Product judgment

**External evidence:** Product Operations guidance from [Atlassian](https://www.atlassian.com/agile/product-management/product-operations) and [Aha!](https://www.aha.io/roadmapping/guide/product-management/what-is-product-operations) connects the role with reliable workflows, useful data, tools, and cross-team alignment.

**RentOk decision:** The APM owns the flow from an understood problem to a checked result. They may recommend and make bounded decisions. Product leadership continues to own major priorities, strategy, large trade-offs, and unsafe or irreversible decisions until the APM has earned wider authority.

**Guardrail:** Do not turn the role into a permanent coordinator or an unsupported "mini PM."

### Using AI in Product work is separate from technical ability

**External evidence:** [OpenAI's AI-ready workforce paper](https://cdn.openai.com/global-affairs/openai-edu-ai-ready-workforce.pdf) describes AI fluency in terms of completing tasks, refining thinking, and improving productivity. [Linear](https://linear.app/ai) and [Notion](https://www.notion.com/en-gb/blog/how-notion-uses-custom-agents) show AI inside normal work such as finding context, handling recurring work, and keeping work current.

**RentOk inference:** Useful AI work is broader than coding. It belongs in everyday Product thinking, writing, checking, organisation, and follow-through.

**RentOk decision:** Assess `Using AI in Product work` as its own capability. Record technical ability separately as an optional signal.

**Guardrail:** Do not reward tool names, prompt vocabulary, or polished output by itself.

### Mature AI experience is useful but is not an automatic hiring gate

**External evidence:** [Intercom](https://www.intercom.com/blog/five-key-takeaways-about-ai-product-management/) emphasises curiosity, critical thinking, and solving a real problem instead of using AI for its own sake. [Atlassian's AI collaboration research](https://www.atlassian.com/blog/ai-at-work/ai-collaboration-report) argues that the way people work with AI matters more than adoption alone.

**RentOk inference:** Current exposure is uneven. Previous access to AI tools should not outweigh strong Product evidence and credible learning ability.

**RentOk decision:** A candidate with limited current AI experience can receive an internship or a direct full-time offer. Judge the whole body of evidence. Refusal to learn, repeated careless use, or avoidance of responsibility is a different concern.

**Guardrail:** Do not use an AI score as a mechanical internship or full-time threshold.

### Assess AI through ordinary Product work

**External evidence:** Linear and Notion place AI inside real workflows rather than treating it as an isolated prompt test. [U.S. Office of Personnel Management guidance](https://www.opm.gov/policy-data-oversight/assessment-and-selection/structured-interviews/) supports asking candidates consistent questions and using the same standards.

**RentOk decision:** Encourage but do not require AI in the standard Product work sample. Ask every candidate for a short working-method note. Use the same guided critique only when AI learning evidence is otherwise missing. Keep the technical exercise separate and optional.

**Guardrail:** Do not require a paid tool, complete prompt history, or a separate prompt-writing examination.

### The human remains responsible

**External evidence:** The [NIST AI Risk Management Framework](https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework) provides a way to manage AI risks to individuals, organisations, and society and to consider trustworthiness when AI is used or evaluated.

**RentOk decision:** AI may help do the work, but it does not own the work. The person who submits, shares, recommends, or acts on an output is responsible for checking it and for what happens next. Checks become stricter as the possible harm increases.

**Guardrail:** Serious dishonesty, unsafe handling of information, or repeated unchecked use affects judgment, reliability, or integrity. Ordinary inexperience remains coachable.

### AI development is expected, but usage is not a KRA

**External evidence:** [Notion's internal agent examples](https://www.notion.com/en-gb/blog/how-notion-uses-custom-agents) and [Atlassian's team workflow guidance](https://www.atlassian.com/blog/teamwork/ai-insights-january-2026) focus on recurring work, shared context, coordination, and outcomes.

**RentOk decision:** Include AI learning in onboarding and the first 90 days. Review useful progress, checking, and accountability. Do not create quotas for prompts, tools, generated documents, or time spent using AI.

**Guardrail:** Limited proficiency alone does not fail conversion or probation. A lack of progress matters when it shows refusal, carelessness, or a wider learning problem.

### Entry route follows the whole body of evidence

**Benchmark limit:** Employment terms should not be copied from another company. The role evidence, candidate's experience, RentOk policy, and applicable employment requirements govern this decision.

**RentOk decision:** Use an internship intended to convert when the candidate has potential but limited evidence of consistent work in a real Product setting. Use direct full-time when repeated professional evidence supports the role, even if AI experience is still developing. Normal probation is the default for direct full-time hires. Any exception follows company policy and needs a clear reason.

**Guardrail:** Do not use probation to avoid assessment or an internship to underlevel an experienced candidate.

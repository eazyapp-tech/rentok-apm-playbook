# Where information lives

Use this page before creating, moving, or linking APM material.

## The rule

GitHub holds reusable knowledge. Notion holds live hiring work and personal information.

Do not make either place a full copy of the other. That creates stale pages and increases the chance of exposing candidate information.

## Put this in GitHub

- the role definition and job description
- the standard assessment process
- blank scorecards and templates
- candidate-facing exercises with fictional data
- onboarding and first 90-day guidance
- working rhythm and decision rights
- general examples with all personal details removed
- change history and review rules

## Keep this in Notion

- recruiter messages and hiring coordination
- candidate names, email addresses, phone numbers, and resumes
- portfolios linked to a candidate record
- completed scorecards and evidence files
- interview notes, references, and recordings
- hiring decisions, compensation, and offer details
- archive copies of earlier operational pages

## When a page exists in both places

Some reusable pages may also be shown in Notion because that is where hiring work happens.

For those pages:

1. Make the approved change in the repository.
2. Update the matching Notion page in the same piece of work.
3. Check that the meaning still matches.
4. Add a short changelog entry when the rule, decision, or expected action changed.

Do not copy candidate information into the repository while updating the shared page.

## Before adding a new page

Ask four questions:

1. Who will use it?
2. What decision or action will it support?
3. Does an existing page already answer this?
4. Could it contain personal, confidential, or time-sensitive information?

If it is reusable and safe, add it here and link it from the main README. If it is a live record about a person or hiring process, keep it in Notion.

# Weekly APM review

Use these five sections for the weekly review with the Product Lead. Keep each point short enough to support a decision or next action.

## Completed

What reached a meaningful result?

-

## Moving

What is progressing normally?

-

## At risk

What may fail or be delayed? State the likely effect and the current response.

-

## Decision needed

Where is the Product Lead's judgment needed? Bring a recommendation when possible.

-

## Learned

What changed our understanding of the problem, user, or solution?

-
